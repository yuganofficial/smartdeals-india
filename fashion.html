💡 Fashion Buying Guide — 2026

  • Size charts are everything: Never order by habit — check the brand's cm/inch size chart on every store.
  • Shoe sizing: Indian brands differ by up to a size; read "fits as expected" review stats before choosing.
  • Fabric tells comfort: For Kerala heat and humidity, rayon, cotton blends and linen beat polyester.
  • Meesho = ethnic wear goldmine: Sarees and kurti sets at ₹300–800 with free returns on most items.
  • Return window: Fashion sizes are risky — prefer products with 7+ day returns and exchange offers.

Fashion FAQs

Which store is cheapest for sarees and kurtis?

Meesho usually wins for everyday ethnic wear with prices from ₹300, while Flipkart and Amazon have better brand options during Big Billion Days and festive sales.

Are budget running shoes durable?

For walking, gym and casual daily use, ₹1,000–1,500 shoes from established brands last 8–12 months easily. For actual long-distance running, invest more.

How do I pick the right sunglasses?

Insist on UV400 protection (blocks 100% UVA/UVB) — cheap sunglasses without UV coating actually harm your eyes. Polarisation is a bonus for driving.
