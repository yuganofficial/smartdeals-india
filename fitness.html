💡 Home Gym Buying Guide — 2026

  • Start with the trio: Yoga mat + resistance bands + skipping rope (~₹1,600 total) covers 80% of beginner workouts.
  • Dumbbell weight: Men: start 2×10kg; women: 2×5kg is plenty for toning routines. Rubber coating protects floors.
  • Band quality: Latex bands last far longer than TPE — check material in the specs before buying.
  • Protein safety: Buy only from official brand stores; check the FSSAI mark and verify authenticity codes on the jar.
  • Consistency beats gear: A ₹300 rope used daily beats a ₹30,000 treadmill used once a month.

Fitness FAQs

Can I build muscle with just resistance bands?

Yes — progressive overload works with bands too. Move to heavier band colours as sets get easier. Many physios recommend bands even for advanced users.

Is 6mm thick enough for a yoga mat?

6mm is the sweet spot for knees on hard floors. 4mm suits balance-heavy yoga; 8mm+ is comfy but can wobble during standing poses.

Do I need whey protein as a beginner?

Not necessarily — eggs, milk, paneer and chicken cover most needs. Whey is a convenient top-up when daily protein is hard to hit with food alone.
