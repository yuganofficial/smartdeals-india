💡 Electronics Buying Guide — 2026

  • Battery claims: Look for 40+ hours earbuds playtime and 20000mAh+ power banks. Check recent reviews to confirm real-world numbers.
  • Type-C everywhere: Prefer products with USB-C charging — faster, reversible and one cable for all devices.
  • Bluetooth 5.0 or above: Gives better range, lower latency and improved battery efficiency.
  • Warranty matters: Choose brands offering at least a 1-year warranty with service centres in India.
  • Certified safe: For chargers and power banks, look for BIS certification — never buy uncertified lithium products.
