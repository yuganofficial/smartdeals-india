💡 Kitchen Buying Guide — 2026

  • Air fryer capacity: 4L suits a family of 4. Smaller 2.5L units are fine for 1–2 people and cost much less.
  • Mixer wattage: 750W handles idli batter and dry spices easily; 500W is okay only for light daily use.
  • Induction compatibility: Check the base symbol — magnetic/cook-ready base works on both gas and induction.
  • Safety first: Kettles and mixers should have auto shut-off and overload protection (IP rating not needed here).
  • Warranty: Prefer 2-year warranty on powered appliances — motor issues show up in year 1–2.
