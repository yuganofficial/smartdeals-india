💡 Beauty Buying Guide — 2026

  • Actives first: For skincare, look for % of actives (Vitamin C 15–20%, Salicylic Acid 1–2%) rather than fancy packaging.
  • Always patch test: Try any new serum or cream on your inner arm for 24 hours before applying to the face.
  • Sunscreen is non-negotiable: SPF 50 PA++++ daily — even indoors near windows and even on cloudy Kerala days.
  • Buy from official stores: Fake cosmetics are a real problem — buy from official brand stores on Amazon/Flipkart or trusted Meesho sellers with high ratings.
  • Check manufacturing date: Beauty products expire (usually 2–3 years unopened, 6–12 months after opening).

Beauty FAQs

Is Vitamin C serum suitable for oily skin?

Yes — choose a water-based or gel serum marked non-comedogenic. Avoid heavy oil-based serums if you get frequent pimples.

Are Meesho beauty products genuine?

Stick to sellers with 4★+ ratings and 500+ reviews, and prefer items marked with brand names. For premium actives, Amazon/Flipkart official brand stores are safer.

Which sunscreen works for humid Kerala weather?

Gel-based or fluid sunscreens marked "no white cast" and "oil-free" feel light in humidity and won't clog pores.
