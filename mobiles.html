💡 Mobile Buying Guide — 2026

  • Processor first: Under ₹15,000, chipset matters more than camera megapixels — it decides speed for years.
  • 5G is standard now: All good phones in this range support 5G; check band support for your operator.
  • Battery trio: 5000mAh + 33W charging + AMOLED is the sweet spot combo under ₹15k.
  • Compare checkout prices: Amazon and Flipkart swap "cheapest" crown weekly — bank-card offers often decide.
  • Protect it day one: A ₹300 case + ₹200 tempered glass saves a ₹3,000 screen repair.

Mobile FAQs

Which is better under ₹15,000 — Amazon or Flipkart?

Both list the same models, but exchange bonuses and card discounts differ. Check both, compare the final "payable" amount, and buy whichever is lower that week.

Are budget phones good for gaming?

Yes, for BGMI/Free Fire at medium settings with 90Hz displays. Look for UFS storage (not eMMC) and a newer 5G chipset for smooth long sessions.

Is buying a phone from Meesho safe?

Stick to listings with 4★+ seller ratings and full brand-name models with warranty cards. For phones, Amazon/Flipkart official stores remain the safest bet.
